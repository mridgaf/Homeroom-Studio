**Era (owner already picked 2026-09-05):** Yeezus, the reduced version.

## What the sources say (Wikipedia: Yeezus, Black Skinhead; plus Tunebat)
- "Abrasive, electronic soundscape with distorted drum machines and synthesizers"; draws on industrial, acid house, electro, punk, Chicago drill, horrorcore (Yeezus) [CONFIRMED, Yeezus + Chicago Tribune via Wikipedia].
- Rick Rubin joined 15 days before release to "strip down" to a "stripped-down minimal direction", often removing elements already recorded (Yeezus, 2 lines) [CONFIRMED]. Example: Bound 2 backing replaced with "a single note bassline in the hook... processed to have a punk edge in the Suicide tradition" [UNSURE one quote].
- Slant: "alien, angular beats, slowly morphing drones and sirens, abrupt periods of silence" [CONFIRMED with the old line's siren and bars that drop out].
- West forbade "bass wobbles" reminiscent of dubstep (Yeezus) [UNSURE one line].
- Black Skinhead: industrial-sounding beat, "rumbling tribal beat" (Digital Spy), "blunt glam-rock drumbeat and distorted synthesizer line" (NYT via Wikipedia), live AND programmed drums by Daft Punk [CONFIRMED]; Rolling Stone "industrial glitter-rock stomp".
- On Sight interpolates "Sermon (He'll Give Us What We Really Need)" by the Holy Name of Mary Choral Family; New Slaves samples Hungarian rock group Omega; I Am a God an obscure Hindi sample (Yeezus) [CONFIRMED].
- Bound 2 "features heavy soul music samples"; the only Yeezus track recalling his early work [CONFIRMED].
