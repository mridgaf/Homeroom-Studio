**Era (owner already picked 2026-09-05, "Nerd"):** the live-band era, N.E.R.D. with Spymob. Glass Cat keeps the Neptunes minimal sound.

## What the sources say
- In Search Of... was re-recorded: "the largely electronic production backing replaced by live backing provided by 1960s-style power pop band Spymob" and a "more rock-oriented sound with the use of live drums and guitars" (Wikipedia, In Search Of... and Spymob pages, plus Rock Star: "rerecorded courtesy of Spymob rather than the Neptunes' trademark synths") [CONFIRMED, 3 pages]. Released worldwide 2002-03-12.
- Chad Hugo "learned to play the guitar" after that and the band "mostly play their own instruments" (Wikipedia) [CONFIRMED].
- Fly or Die (2004): Pharrell "just started playing guitar last year"; Spymob helped on instruments; "art rock", XTC/10cc comparisons; "smooth-jazz licks" (Wikipedia, reviews) [CONFIRMED].
- Seeing Sounds (2008): "combining a classic rock sound with a 1970s funk swing" (Hugo via Garland); drums by bucket or drum machine first, then live band fills the song; order: drum programming, vocals, synths, live drums, live guitar, then the rest; Korg 01/W triggering Triton Extreme [UNSURE one blog + Remix interview]; "Laugh About It": bass programmed through the Triton, then live guitar; "Yea You": built on a keyboard then replayed live; saxophone inspiration (Gary Bartz) in the Remix piece [UNSURE].
- In the electronic In Search Of... version a saxophone plays at the end of an instrumental (Wikipedia) [UNSURE].
