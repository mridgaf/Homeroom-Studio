## What the sources say
- Slows songs to "60 and 70 quarter-note beats per minute", with beat-skipping, scratching, stop-time (Wikipedia) [CONFIRMED]. Slow pitch gives "a mellow, heavy sound that emphasized lyrics" [CONFIRMED]. Started his slowed mixes in 1990 (Wikipedia); the tempo IS the style (matches the line).
- Screw scratched Z.Z. Hill/B.B. King/Johnnie Taylor-type records by slowing them (DJ Screw page) [UNSURE]; he took piano lessons but was uninspired (DJ Screw page).
- Spice 1's "Welcome to the Ghetto" screwed over a Marvin Gaye "Inner City Blues" sample (Micro-Chop) [UNSURE].
- "Rich with slow-winding melodies similar to that of the West Coast G-Funk" (Bitter Southerner) [UNSURE one article].
- Houston rap was slower from the start (Geto Boys), unlike upbeat Miami bass and Memphis (Wikipedia) [CONFIRMED].
