## Gear and method
FL Studio with custom VSTs, also Maschine and Pro Tools (Wikipedia); earlier MPC 2000 / 2500 [CONFIRMED]. Lead melody first, drums after; layers orchestral instruments and "tubular bells, a key aspect of his sound"; Note Repeat for hi-hat rolls; mixer automation recorded live (Novation, Luger himself) [CONFIRMED by HexLoops summary + Novation]. Church drummer (Wikipedia, HexLoops) [CONFIRMED].

## Drums
"Jackhammer drum", "hard-hitting 808 kick drums", "crisp snare drums", "frantic synthesizers" (Wikipedia); "hard, aggressive, simple, not too much" (Luger, Novation) [CONFIRMED]; fast hi-hat rolls, clipped claps and snares (HexLoops) [UNSURE]. "Extra snare fills every 4th bar", "zero drop-outs", "stacked snare+clap" in the current line were NOT re-found; carried over.

## Instruments, ranked

2. **Tubular bells** [CONFIRMED by Luger himself, Novation].
3. **Spooky synths** [CONFIRMED]: "spooky trap synth" (Wikipedia), "dense synthesizers" (Flockaveli), "ominous synth lines".
