## What the sources say
- In-house producer for Roc-A-Fella in the early 2000s, "developing the 'chipmunk soul' sampling style" (Wikipedia, RouteNote) [CONFIRMED].
- "Renowned for layering and stacking additional instrumentation over [samples], giving the music a much bigger, more epic sound" (RouteNote) [UNSURE one source].
- Drums "have a distinctly live feel, incorporating percussion and layered hand claps"; plays drums himself and adds live fills (RouteNote) [UNSURE one source]. Distinctive synth FX riser combined with his tag (RouteNote).
- Gear: ASR-10 first (mid-90s, because RZA used one; Just Blaze, Tape Op) [CONFIRMED by himself]; MPC 4000 for some; an early DAW user (RouteNote) [UNSURE].
- Family: mother a singer, father a jazz organist; he played piano/organ as a kid (Tape Op, RouteNote) [CONFIRMED].
- Vocal samples chosen to carry a message, "built-in hook" (Tape Op) [CONFIRMED by himself].
- Not re-found: "huge snare-clap stack", "busy driving hats", "crash swells", "leaning forward like a parade" (layered claps and live feel partly support).
