## What the sources say
- ZUU's "steroidal 808s and air siren synths, inspired equally by 2 Live Crew and XXXTentacion" (Highsnobiety) [UNSURE one article; matches the line's "steroidal, distorted 808" and "air-raid-siren-style fx"].
- ZUU "went straight off feel. There was not a lot of overthinking at all" (Finatik, Highsnobiety); TA13OO took "a really meticulous approach". ZUU made in under four months, a fraction of the time of TA13OO (Zac, Complex) [CONFIRMED by two interviews].
- "SHAKE 88" built on a sample of MC Cool Rock & MC Chaszy Chess's 1988 Miami bass record "Boot The Booty" (Highsnobiety) [UNSURE]. "BIRDZ" built on a field recording with drums put on it. "P.A.T." blends XXXTentacion and SpaceGhostPurrp.
- Roles: "Finatik was doing more of the drums and I was doing more of the keys... now we both do everything" (Zac). Tools: MPC 2000, FL Studio, Reason (Complex). Zac took piano lessons as a kid.
- Producer tag "FNZ, well damn!" (Highsnobiety) [CONFIRMED], used all over ZUU.
- Not found: tempos, keys, the clap "with a little wander", "driving 16th hats", "cowbell riding through".
