## Gear
- Danja: MPC3000 + virtual synths on a Mac (SexyBack, Wikipedia); Timbaland: ASR-10 on SexyBack [CONFIRMED by Wikipedia]. So the ASR-10 is NOT Chrome Dial's alone; the old line's "NOT the ASR-10" is wrong for SexyBack. The era split rests on the club-synth sound, not on the sampler.
- Mix: Pro Tools; drums printed to half-inch and back "to make them sound punchier"; kick on two 1176s; snare "fat but cuts through" (Araica, SoS) [CONFIRMED with the old note]. Round, big kick "with no decay... just a punch" (SoS).
- Araica's reference for the kick: Benny Benassi "Satisfaction" (SoS) [CONFIRMED one person].
