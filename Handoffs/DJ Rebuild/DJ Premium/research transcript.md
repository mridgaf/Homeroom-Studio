## Gear and era marker
- MPC60 only triggers drums; samples live in the S950 (60 seconds max); everything including keyboards MIDI'd to the MPC; Technics turntable feeds the S950 (Sound On Sound, Premier himself) [CONFIRMED].
- Then everything goes into Pro Tools and he shapes the final sound on a Control 24; "full-scale digital in 2005"; "you can still get grimy and gritty with Pro Tools, it's all about knowing how to dust it up a bit" (SoS) [CONFIRMED for 2005+; before that, reel-to-reel].

## Drums, feel
- "Turn off the 16th notes and play the MPC live so it sounds like live drumming... I like it to sound loose... a little bounce" (SoS) [CONFIRMED with the old note].
- Characterised by short sampled loops, hard-hitting drums, deep bass lines, "slightly behind-the-beat groove" (SoS) [CONFIRMED].
- "The sample is mellow, but the drums are still smacking" (SoS).
- Moment of Truth reviewers: breaks "dry and crisp", "no heavy-handed 808s" (Off Your Radar, one writer) [UNSURE one source].
- Swing %: only the old RouteNote 51-54 (not reopened, page 404 today). Premier gives none. NOT FOUND from Premier. The 53 stays as the engine's number.

## Instruments, ranked
1. **Horns / brass stabs** [CONFIRMED, 4 sources]: Aguilera track is built on a brass sample and a stab riff (Wikipedia, SoS "the drum pattern and the stabs"); Moment of Truth has "punctuated horn tracks" (Off Your Radar); RBMA: "I started doing stabs then everybody started getting into that" (stab = short dash, strip down); Aguilera asked him to "add some horn parts" (RBMA).
2. **Piano / electric piano** [CONFIRMED]: Work, Robbin Hood Theory, Royalty clicked together "on the strength of playful piano loops"; "flowing, cool electric piano" (Off Your Radar, two reviewers); Pitchfork snippet "plinky piano"; Premier took piano lessons and plays "my own little chords now, my own basslines" on keyboards (RBMA, SoS).
3. **Strings** [CONFIRMED]: Nas Is Like choppy string sample (Wikipedia); oompa strings on Rep Growz Bigga (Off Your Radar).
4. **Bass** [CONFIRMED]: SoS "deep bass lines"; "if the low end of the sample isn't heavy, I follow the exact bass line and put that underneath". Off Your Radar: "alternating bass" on Robbin Hood Theory.
5. Harps / chimes (In Memory Of), bird chirps (Nas Is Like), scratches [CONFIRMED]: not engine words, left out of the line.
6. Guitar: Premier plays guitar (SoS) but no track in this era names it. Left at 0.
