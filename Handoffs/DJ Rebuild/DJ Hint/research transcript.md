## Samples (what the records were built from) [CONFIRMED, Wikipedia: The Professional + I Like Control]

## Mix and arrangement
- Duro: mixing is "about balance: moving levels up and down, panning left and right." [UNSURE, SonicScoop]
- Duro: often skips EQ/compression; compression "from a tape machine." [UNSURE, SonicScoop]
- Tapes: rough. "Nobody really mixed these records, all rough." (blogger, 1997 tape) [UNSURE]
- Clue talked over songs on tapes; two songs, his voice over them, on Hot 97 in two weeks. [UNSURE, Hip Hop Scriptures quoting Drink Champs]
- Tapes lean on R&B blends, chopped vocals over classic beats (Winter 1, 1995) [UNSURE, one blog]
- Freestyles made for the tape, over other records' instrumentals. [UNSURE, one blog]
- Voice drops: "Clue" call, evil laugh, "Cluminati" [UNSURE, search summaries of Billboard and AceShowbiz; I did NOT open those pages]. Scratching: **NOT FOUND**. The old stamp (scratch/siren) stands in for a vocal drop and has no source.

## Who carries it
- Ken "Duro" Ifill: engineer, mixer, co-producer on nearly every Clue-credited track; lifelong friend and business partner since age 13. [CONFIRMED: Complex, Wikipedia, Roland]
- Clue: curator and voice. Funkmaster Flex: "He's a mixtape DJ and he's a great producer." [UNSURE, one secondary page]
- The Professional is multi-producer (Buckwild, DJ Scratch, Hangmen 3, M.O.P., Laze-E-Laze, V.I.C., Swizz). No single house sound. [CONFIRMED]
