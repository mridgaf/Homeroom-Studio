## Scope (ASSUMING, owner not asked; correct any)
Horror Rap = the 1991-95 horrorcore canon in three regional strands:
- Houston: Geto Boys, "Mind Playing Tricks on Me" and "Chuckie" (1991, Scarface and others).
- New York: Gravediggaz, *6 Feet Deep* (1994, Prince Paul with RZA).
- Sacramento: Brotha Lynch Hung, *Season of da Siccness* (1995, Kevin Mann himself).
The tracker names "Gravediggaz / Brotha Lynch / the horrorcore lane". Why this cut:
- Esham (Detroit, 1989-94) is already **Acid Rap Detroit** (guitar/rock, 98 BPM).
- Three 6 Mafia (Memphis, 140 BPM) is **Memphis**. Flatlinerz not used (see sources: only "Live Evil", producer DR Period, found).
- G-funk is its own genre. Brotha Lynch is the border case: one German encyclopedia summary calls Siccness horrorcore with G-funk elements [UNSURE, one source].

# Part 2 of 3: sound, drums, instruments, bass, mix

## What the sources say about the sound, by claim
- **Dark.** Prince Paul on playing the beats to the group: "all of the music was just dark" (Coleman, 2016, his own words). Rolling Stone: production "evokes the atmosphere of horror movies and ominous effects" (via Wikipedia); Quietus: "ominous beats"; Time Is Illmatic: "dark, raw, and minimal" for 2 Cups of Blood. [CONFIRMED, 4+ sources]
- **Minor.** Entertainment Weekly (via Wikipedia): "washed in minor chords". Keys above are 5 of 6 minor. [CONFIRMED minor]. Harmonic minor, phrygian: NOT FOUND.
- **Mid-tempo.** Q magazine (via Wikipedia): "a chilling mid-tempo stomp". Time Is Illmatic: "dim mid-tempo instrumental". [CONFIRMED, plus the BPM table]
- **Sparse / minimal.** Time Is Illmatic on 2 Cups of Blood; Albumism calls one Geto Boys song "the most sonically sparse". Only one is about these beats. [UNSURE]
- **Dusty drums.** Albumism and Time Is Illmatic both say it of RZA's Graveyard Chamber ("dusty drum track"/"dusty drums"). Time Is Illmatic also: "rough drums" (Rest in Peace outro), "off beat drums" (6 Feet Deep). Apple Music editorial: "creaky beats". [CONFIRMED dusty for RZA-made tracks; the Prince Paul tracks are not described]
- **Scratchy source.** KUTX: "raw vinyl scratches in the sample" on MPTOM. Diary's Johnny Mathis loop came off a cassette (Reverb, AltCitizen, the claaa7 article, all citing the story). [CONFIRMED a cassette-sourced loop on Diary; vinyl scratches UNSURE, one source]. Tape hiss on the whole genre: NOT FOUND.
- **Reverb.** NOT FOUND. The old note used EW's "washed in minor chords" to justify a long dark reverb ("washed" space) and the old tests repeat it. That sentence is about the chords. Nothing found says the snare is drowned in reverb.
