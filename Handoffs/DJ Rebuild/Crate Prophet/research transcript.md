# Drums, swing, grid
- Sampler: E-mu SP-1200 for everything "back in the day" (Pete, Village Voice; Wikipedia says all productions until 2003) and an Akai S950 (Complex, RouteNote: SP-1200 triggers S950) [CONFIRMED]. SP-1200 sound described as "crunchy digitized drums" (Village Voice / Detrick, XXL, RapReviews) [CONFIRMED]. Limited sample time (about 10 s) forced chopping (VV).
- Mecca era: heavily layered drums, built on Mountain "Long Red" break; hi-hat locked to eighth notes. Main Ingredient: lighter, Brethren "Outside Love" break (RouteNote) [UNSURE, one source]. Main Ingredient drums "snappy and crisp" (Wikipedia) [UNSURE].
- Gritty drums (Wikipedia, RouteNote) [CONFIRMED]. Space left between sample, kick and snare (HexLoops; Wikipedia "spacious grooves") [CONFIRMED].
- Drums come from breaks and layering: Marley Marl "snare from this record, a kick from that record" shaped him (Wax Poetics); World Is Yours drums from a Rimshots break; "Letter to the Future" from Black Heat "Zimba Ku" (Wikipedia). No 808 or synth drum is named anywhere in these sources.
- Jam Master Jay played the drum pattern on "Down With the King" (1993, DJBooth) [UNSURE, one source].
- Swing: NOT FOUND from Pete or an engineer. RouteNote says his hats were rigidly quantised [UNSURE]. A MPC-Forums poster says Pete and Premier "def using quantize" [UNSURE, anecdote]. A Gearspace poster programs double tempo with swing near 71% for the "Pete Rock feel" [UNSURE, forum]. RapReviews: SP-1200 is "firm but with plenty of bounce". So "60% golden-era swing" in the old line has NO direct source and one source against it for the hats.
- Snare late/early: NOT FOUND.
- Congas: NOT FOUND for the 1991-94 work. (Pete adds tambourine/percussion on Petestrumentals 3, 2020; out of era.)

# Instruments, bass, mix
1. **Horns / sax** (Tom Scott sax on T.R.O.Y.; horns on Shut 'Em Down, Straighten It Out era; "echoed one-shots"; Exclaim "echoing horns", Wikipedia "swirling horns") [CONFIRMED, 5+ sources]. Pete: sax "hit me right in the chest" (DJBooth). He found the horns deeper in the Tom Scott record than Black Sheep had (Wax Poetics).
2. **Piano / keys**: Ahmad Jamal piano (World Is Yours, Ethan Hein + Wikipedia); Rhodes only on Juicy remix (Complex); "filtered keyboard loop" (HexLoops) [CONFIRMED piano; Rhodes UNSURE].
3. **Bass**: resonant basslines (Wikipedia, Exclaim "bass-flooded"). Early: filtered sampled bass, made from the horn record on T.R.O.Y. ("I filtered it to make the bass line", Complex). Pete: "I make all my basslines up myself" (Unkut) and played live electric bass later (RouteNote) [CONFIRMED]. Sound is a played, melodic, filtered line, not a fixed pulse.
4. Vocal sample (Harvey Scales "Sun Won't Come Out", Wikipedia) [UNSURE, one track]. Vibraphone-type sound (Roy Ayers on Lots of Lovin') [UNSURE: instrument inferred from the artist, not stated].
5. Guitar/strings: Curtis Mayfield live on "The Joy" (2010, out of era); NOT FOUND in 1991-94.
- Layering and chopping several records in one song (Wikipedia, RouteNote) [CONFIRMED]. Hazy, droning sound "resembles a vinyl record" (Wikipedia) [UNSURE one source]. Recorded at Greene St., Manhattan; its EQ gave a wah-wah effect (Wikipedia) [UNSURE].
- Long intro/outro beats: short unrelated excerpts (Wikipedia; Main Ingredient interludes). The engine has no sections; left out.
- Chords/key: no published progressions (Ethan Hein links an image transcription we cannot read) NOT FOUND. Keys from tools: A#m x2, Em, D, B, C [UNSURE, tools disagree].
- Sidechain: NOT FOUND (SP-1200 era has no sidechain; the 1-in-10 skip is the owner's rule).

# Who worked with him
- **Jamey Staub** (engineer, Soul Survivor II): "Once Pete has a basic beat going... he'll start to arrange it" (MPC Forums repost; Wikipedia) [UNSURE: repost, 2004-era].
- **Eddie F** (Heavy D's DJ) showed him the sampler (Pete, Unkut + VV).
- **Heavy D / Marley Marl**: Pete watched them work (VV); Marley's snare-from-here, kick-from-there method shaped him (Wax Poetics).
- **Large Professor**: finished T.R.O.Y. at his house on an SP-950; Pete wrote bass and drums on "In The World" (Complex).
- **Jam Master Jay**: played drums on "Down With the King" (DJBooth).
- **Q-Tip**: re-created the "Jazz (We Got)" beat; credits "Pete Rock for the beat" in the outro (Wikipedia / Complex).
- **CL Smooth** (The Source 1992 via Hip Hop Nostalgia): the beat "will draw them into the lyrics". 
- **Ben Detrick** (Village Voice writer), AllMusic's Stanton Swihart: "among some of the most seductive in hip-hop" production.
- Quote hunt result: no producer quote describing his drum swing; no direct Large Pro or Common quote about his style was found.
