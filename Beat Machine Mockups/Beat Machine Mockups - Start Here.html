Homeroom Studio (Beat Machine) — 3 front end mockups

Click each to open it in its own tab. Same content (The Crew, Legends, Styles, batch controls, results) shown in three different layouts so the page doesn't feel like one long scroll. Look-only — buttons don't do anything.

A — Tabbed Poster Keeps your exact brand (blue board, yellow accents, condensed type) but splits the long page into 3 tabs: Pick a sound, Library & breaks, This batch. B — Step Wizard Phone-app feel. One decision per screen (DJ → style → tempo/directions → go), progress dots up top, results at the bottom of the flow. C — Studio Dashboard Light mode, three-column layout: section nav on the left, picker/controls in the middle, results always visible on the right — no scrolling to see what you made.
