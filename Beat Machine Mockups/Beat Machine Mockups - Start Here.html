Homeroom Studio (Beat Machine) — 7 front end mockups

Click each to open it in its own tab. Same content (The Crew, Legends, Styles, batch controls, results) shown in different layouts and looks. Look-only — buttons don't do anything.

A — Tabbed Poster Keeps your exact brand (blue board, yellow accents, condensed type) but splits the long page into 3 tabs: Pick a sound, Library & breaks, This batch. B — Step Wizard Phone-app feel. One decision per screen (DJ → style → tempo/directions → go), progress dots up top, results at the bottom of the flow. C — Studio Dashboard Light mode, three-column layout: section nav on the left, picker/controls in the middle, results always visible on the right — no scrolling to see what you made. D — Show Flyer Yellow paper + blue ink, halftone texture, hand-cut sticker cards, your logo as a rotated sticker. Poster energy, not a screen-generic look. E — Rack Unit Dark hardware fascia, blue-lit screens, glowing yellow LED readouts, your logo as an equipment badge. Feels like gear, not an app. F — Sticker Sheet Same flyer family as D. Light graph paper, punch-out cards with dashed die-cut lines, slight rotation per card, logo as a peel-off sticker. G — Screen Print Poster Same flyer family as D, inverted: bold blue field, offset-print yellow ink, halftone texture, striped border frame like a gig poster.
