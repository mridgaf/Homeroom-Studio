# Eerie Music-Box Trap — sounds like JID "NEVER" (The Never Story)

**Accuracy: C** — built by ear from the album, not from an interview or stem
breakdown. The devices and moves below get you the *shape* of the Never
Story sound: eerie toy-box melody, sliding sub, and a beat with holes in it.

## The Chain
Klang Tuned Percussion (music-box patch) → The Echo (dark ping-pong) → RV7000 (small dark plate) + SubTractor 808 (portamento) + sparse Kong kit through ReGroove → dry upfront rap vocal

## Steps
1. The melody: Klang Tuned Percussion, pick a music-box / celesta-type patch. Write a 4-bar minor-key line high up (C5-C6), only 5–7 notes, with at least one flat-2 or tritone in it — pretty but wrong is the target. If a Klang patch search comes up empty, an NN-XT celesta or mallet patch does the same job.
2. Detune the innocence away: clone the Klang track, drop the copy 12 semitones and nudge its fine-tune −10 cents. Mix the low copy 6 dB under the top one. The slight beating between them is the "eerie" part.
3. Space: The Echo after Klang — 1/4 note, feedback ~35%, ping-pong ON, filter the repeats dark (LP around 2 kHz). The delay tail must be doing more work than the melody.
4. The 808: SubTractor sine (Osc 1 waveform 4), Polyphony 1, Legato ON, Portamento 45–55. Play it on the Launchkey KEYS, low octave: hold the first note and press the next one WHILE still holding — that overlap is what makes it slide. Write the bassline so at least one note per bar slides a fourth or more. (Same engine as the Trap 808 recipe; the Never Story flavor is longer slides, fewer notes.)
5. Drums with holes: Kong or Redrum. Finger them on the Launchkey PADS (Shift + Pad Mode → Drum, kick at bottom-left) instead of mouse-clicking — record a loose take, then delete hits until only 3 or 4 kicks per 2 bars remain, never on every downbeat. Snare or rim on 3 (half-time feel). Hats: closed 8ths but MUTE 2–3 of them per bar. The silence is where JID's triplet flows live.
6. Groove: whole drum bus through one ReGroove channel, shuffle 55–60%, slide a few ticks late. Loose, not drunk.
7. Glue and grit: drum bus → Scream 4, Tape, Damage 15–20. MClass Compressor on the mix bus, 4:1, ~2 dB reduction.
8. Vocal: dry and dead-center (Upfront Rap Vocal recipe). Double only the last word of every 4th bar, panned hard both sides — the Never Story ad-lib move.

## Why this works
Every element is small: tiny melody, few drums, one sliding sub. Fast, dense rapping needs a sparse beat the way a solo needs a rhythm section — the vocal IS the busy part, and the eerie toy-box top keeps the mood dark without filling the midrange the voice needs.

## Order of operations
Melody first (it sets the key for the 808 slides), then the 808 line, then drums LAST — place kicks only where the bass isn't sliding.

## Reason-specific trick
Klang is the platform advantage: it's a physical-model of exactly the struck-metal timbres this era of Atlanta production sampled. Save Klang + The Echo + RV7000 as a Combinator ("Music Box Dark.cmb") — that's your Never Story template starter.

## RE upgrade path
None needed — all stock Reason 10+ devices. If you own a granular RE, a frozen grain pad 20 dB under everything adds the album's "haunted room" floor.

## Technique you just learned
Negative space: mute drum hits instead of adding them — a hole in the beat is an instrument when the rapper is fast enough to fill it.
