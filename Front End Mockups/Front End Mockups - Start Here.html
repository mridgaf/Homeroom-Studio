Reason Voice — 7 front end mockups

Click each to open it in its own tab. Same recipe (Driven Rock Bass) shown in different layouts/looks so you can compare directly. These are look-only — the buttons don't do anything.

A — Refined Console Your current dark/yellow theme, but tightened up: clearer hierarchy, less crowded sidebar, current step highlighted. B — Single-Focus App Phone-app feel. One big card at a time, recipe list as horizontal chips up top, big round mic button, progress bar for the walkthrough. C — Modern Dashboard Light mode, SaaS-style (like Notion/Linear). Two-column layout: steps as a numbered timeline on the left, chain + notes on the right. D — Field Notebook Cream paper, blue ruled lines, current step marked in yellow highlighter, your logo as a stamped corner mark. Feels handwritten, not templated. E — On Air Radio-console look: blue fascia header, glowing "on air" pill, pulsing yellow hold-to-talk button, your logo as a station badge. F — Legal Pad Same notebook family as D. Yellow legal-pad paper, blue rule lines, handwritten header, your logo taped in the corner like a photo. G — Recipe Index Card Same notebook family as D. Card-catalog look with ring-binder holes and tab dividers, typewriter font, your logo as a round ink stamp.
