Driven Rock Bass

sounds like Royal Blood / Muse — distorted bass that still has low end
Rock Accuracy B
  1. 1
    Split the bass into "Bass Sub" and "Bass Dirt".
  2. 2
    Bass Sub: MClass EQ, cut above ~250 Hz. Keep clean.
  3. 3
    Bass Dirt: MClass EQ hi-pass, cut below 250 Hz.
  4. 4
    Bass Dirt: Scream 4, Damage type Distortion, 55–70.
  5. 5
    Balance dirt path about equal with sub path.
  6. 6
    Merge to bass bus, compressor 4:1, ~3 dB reduction.

The Chain

Spider Audio split → Path A: MClass EQ (lows) → Path B: Scream 4 + MClass EQ (mids/highs) → merge → channel comp

Why this works

Distortion destroys low frequencies (intermodulation mud); splitting first means the sub never touches the dirt.
