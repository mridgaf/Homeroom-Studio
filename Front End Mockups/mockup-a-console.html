Driven Rock Bass

ROCK B

sounds like Royal Blood / Muse — distorted bass that still has low end

The Chain

Spider Audio split → Path A: MClass EQ (lows) → Path B: Scream 4 + MClass EQ (mids/highs) → merge → channel comp

Steps — on step 3 of 6

  1. Split the bass with a Spider Audio into "Bass Sub" and "Bass Dirt".
  2. Bass Sub: MClass EQ, cut everything above ~250 Hz. Keep this path clean.
  3. Bass Dirt: MClass EQ hi-pass, cut below 250 Hz.
  4. Bass Dirt: Scream 4 after the EQ, Damage type Distortion, 55–70.
  5. Balance dirt path about equal with sub path.
  6. Merge to a bass bus, compressor 4:1, ~3 dB reduction.
