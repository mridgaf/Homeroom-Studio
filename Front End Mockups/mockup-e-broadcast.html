Driven Rock Bass

ROCKACCURACY B

sounds like Royal Blood / Muse — distorted bass that still has low end

The Chain

Spider Audio split → Path A: MClass EQ (lows) → Path B: Scream 4 + MClass EQ (mids/highs) → merge → channel comp

Steps — 3 of 6

  1. 1 Split the bass into "Bass Sub" and "Bass Dirt".
  2. 2 Bass Sub: MClass EQ, cut above ~250 Hz. Keep clean.
  3. 3 Bass Dirt: MClass EQ hi-pass, cut below 250 Hz.
  4. 4 Bass Dirt: Scream 4, Damage type Distortion, 55–70.
  5. 5 Balance dirt path about equal with sub path.
  6. 6 Merge to bass bus, compressor 4:1, ~3 dB reduction.
