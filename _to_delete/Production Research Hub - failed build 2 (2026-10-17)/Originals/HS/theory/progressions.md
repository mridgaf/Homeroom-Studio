# Progressions (feelings over time)

A **progression** is a sequence of chords. **Roman numerals** name which step
of the scale each chord is built on — capital letters = major chord, lowercase
= minor chord, a "b" in front = shifted one key flatter (lower). Everything
below is spelled out in **A minor** because A minor is all white keys — easy
to find on the Launchkey. In A minor: i = Am, III = C, iv = Dm, v/V = Em/E,
VI = F, VII = G.

## Grouped by feeling

**Sad, accepting** — i–VI–III–VII → **Am–F–C–G**. The "sad anthem" loop
(*Zombie*, *Numb*). Melancholy that still keeps moving forward.

**Sad, sinking** — i–iv–VI–V → **Am–Dm–F–E**. That last E is a major chord
borrowed from a darker scale — it's the knife-twist of drama in the loop.

**Uplifting** — I–V–vi–IV → in C: **C–G–Am–F**. THE pop "triumph" loop, used
in hundreds of hits. It's the happy cousin of the sad-accepting loop above.

**Nostalgic (borrowed minor)** — IV–iv–I → in C: **F–Fm–C**. That Fm is
borrowed from the minor world for one chord — an instant wistful sigh
(Radiohead/Beatles trick).

**Nostalgic (jazz handshake)** — ii–V–I → in C: **Dm7–G7–Cmaj7**. Smooth
"coming home" motion. Backbone of jazz, soul, and lofi.

**Epic** — i–VII–VI(–VII) → **Am–G–F–G**. A marching descent — trailer
music, stadium-minor energy.

**Dark/menacing** — i–bII → **Am–Bb**. Home chord straight up to the chord
one key above it — pure menace. Drill/horrorcore staple.

**Dreamy** — Imaj7–IVmaj7 on loop → **Cmaj7–Fmaj7**. Two floaty chords
forever = lofi heaven.

**Hip hop / neo-soul (two-chord vamps)** — these never fully resolve, which
is the point: they leave room for the voice.
- i–iv7: **Am–Dm7**
- i–VI: **Am–F**
- ii7–V7 loop (suspended chill, never resolves): **Dm7–G7** forever
- i–v7: **Am–Em7**
- Trap dark-loop favorites: i–VI–III–VII and i–VI–iv–V (Metro Boomin style)

## Use it on the Launchkey

Loop 2 bars of one of these (white keys only, in A minor) before adding any
melody — the progression IS the mood. Rap needs harmony that sits back and
doesn't compete with the vocal, so the two-chord vamps above are your
default; save the 4-chord "epic" and "sad anthem" loops for hooks and choruses.
